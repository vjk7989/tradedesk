## Why
Smoke steps 4–5 place paper orders, and the risk engine refuses them outside NSE hours ("risk denied: market closed for NSE"). Smoke therefore fails at night and on weekends, so it can't be used as a check at any hour.

## Files you may touch
- `e2e/clock_shift.cjs` (new): a preload loaded with `--require`.
- `e2e/smoke.mjs`
- `e2e/README.md`

**Nothing in `trade-mcp/src`, `agent-runner/src` or `bt-service`.** No env var or option that changes the clock may be added to production code.

## What to build
1. **`e2e/clock_shift.cjs`.** At load, it computes a fixed offset so that "now" becomes a fixed trading moment:
   - the moment is a Wednesday at 11:00 Asia/Kolkata;
   - it is not a date in the `marketHolidays` of the risk config smoke uses (check smoke's risk config, and pick a date that is not a holiday);
   - it then patches the global `Date` so `Date.now()`, `new Date()` with no args and `Date()` return real time + offset, with time still advancing;
   - `new Date(x)` with args is unchanged, and `instanceof Date` still works;
   - `performance.now()` and timers are untouched.
   Keep it small (≤40 lines) and commented as test-only.
2. **`smoke.mjs`.** Pass the preload ONLY to the spawned trade-mcp process: `[--require, <abs path to e2e/clock_shift.cjs>, dist/src/index.js]` in its args, not via NODE_OPTIONS on the parent env. It must not reach fake-openalgo, bt-service, the lint step or agent-runner `--check`.
3. **Fold in the #37 review LOWs.**
   - Step 13: also assert the lint output contains "risk config OK".
   - Step 14:
     - the passing run asserts the output contains "MCP_TOKEN: set";
     - the unset-env run asserts the anchored `MCP_TOKEN: missing`, not a loose "missing".
     Match the exact strings printed by `agent-runner/src/main.ts` and `trade-mcp/src/risk/lint.ts`; grep them.
4. **README.** Note that trade-mcp runs under a shifted clock in smoke, and why.

## Acceptance criteria
- `node e2e/smoke.mjs` passes 14/14 at the real wall-clock time, whatever the hour and day.
- Prove that steps 4–5 still exercise the market-hours check. Temporarily point the preload at a Saturday, run, and see steps 4–5 fail with "market closed". Revert, and record this in the report only.
