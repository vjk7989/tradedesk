## What to build
An operator guide, built from `paper-bot/` (PB-1, merged as #97) and the deploy wiring from #95. Every env var, default, endpoint and message must be quoted from the code, with file:line in the report.
- **What it does:**
  - SMA crossover on Delta India public candles;
  - simulated fills with fees and slippage;
  - PAPER ONLY, with no keys and no real orders.
- **Every env knob:** its default and allowed range (the startup validation).
- **How to read the dashboard:** the cards, positions, the equity curve, decisions (actions and reasons), trades, and the filters.
- **Operations:**
  - dashboard login with DASH_USER and a single-quoted DASH_PASSWORD_HASH;
  - how to generate the hash;
  - restart and persistence;
  - resetting the paper account (stop, delete the volume, start);
  - logs.
- **Limits:**
  - closed candles only;
  - candle-aligned ticks;
  - what happens on data errors (skip rows).
- **Not financial advice.** Moving to live needs OpenAlgo plus the runbook §10 checklist.
