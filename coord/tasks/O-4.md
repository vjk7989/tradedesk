# O-4 · trade-mcp · bound the size of audited entries
Branch: own/O-4        Base: main        Owner: OWNER

## Why
The O-3 review noted (LOW, pre-existing) that `appendAudit` writes whatever the caller passes. On the error path, the raw `args` from the AI are unvalidated and unbounded. Redaction only works by key name. A huge or deeply nested argument bloats the audit file and the `audit.recent` window, and can push real entries out of the 1 MB tail.

## Files you may touch
- `trade-mcp/src/risk/audit.ts`: the `redact` pass only.
- `trade-mcp/test/o4_audit_bounds.test.ts` (new)

## What to build
Extend the existing `redact()` into a single "sanitize" pass that keeps today's key-name redaction and adds:
- strings longer than 2000 chars are cut to 2000 + `…[+N chars]`;
- arrays longer than 100 items keep the first 100, plus a marker `"…[+N items]"`;
- depth over 8 becomes `"[depth limit]"`;
- cycles can't occur from JSON args, but guard anyway (a WeakSet, giving `"[cycle]"`);
- a final serialized line over 64 KB is replaced by `{ ts, run, truncated: true, bytes: <original> }`, keeping the `ts` and `run` fields if present.

Normal entries must be byte-identical to today's output. Existing audit tests stay green and unedited.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every limit has a test exactly at, one below and one above the boundary.
- Key-name redaction still applies at every depth.
- A normal order entry is unchanged (snapshot against current output).
- `audit.recent` still parses the truncated entries.
