- `bt-service/uv.lock`: regenerate with the repo's uv (`uv lock`). It must keep **lock revision 3**, the format main uses; don't upgrade the lock format. ccxt stays at 4.5.94, and no other package may move. Check this with a diff.

## Acceptance criteria

- The full bt-service suite is green, and CI is green.
