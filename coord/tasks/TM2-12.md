# TM2-12 · agent-runner · upper bound for `--next N`
Branch: tm2/TM2-12        Base: main (latest)        Owner: TM2

From the #21 edge tests: `--next` has no upper limit, so a huge N loops for a long time or floods the output.

## Files you may touch
- `agent-runner/src/main.ts`: the `--next` argument check only.
- `agent-runner/test/tm2_12_next_bound.test.ts` (new)

## Build
Accept integers from 1 to 100. Anything above 100 exits 1 with `--next N must be between 1 and 100`.

## Acceptance criteria
- 100 works. 101, 1e6 and `Infinity` give that error. Existing `--next` and `--check` tests stay green, unedited.
