## What to build
When `notify.telegram` is set, POST to `https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/sendMessage`:
- the token comes from env `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`;
- `chat_id` comes from the env var named by `chatIdEnv`;
- the text is the job name, status, duration and the first 3500 characters of the result.

Rules:
- Redact the MCP token and the bot token everywhere, in the message and in logs.
- A Telegram failure is logged (redacted) and never fails the job or stops the scheduler.
- Use `fetch` with a 10 s timeout. No new dependency.

## Acceptance criteria
- A fake fetch asserts the URL, the request body and the 3500-character truncation.
- No token appears in any message or log.
- Errors, 4xx/5xx responses and timeouts don't change the job status.
- A job with no `notify` sends nothing.
- `jobs.example.json` still parses. You may add `notify` to one example job, using env var names only.
