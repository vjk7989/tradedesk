- Make every time-dependent decision deterministic. Set the lock mtime explicitly (`utimesSync`) right before each check: "fresh" is mtime = now − 1 s, and "stale" is mtime = now − (LIVE_WINDOW_MS + 5 s). Never rely on real elapsed time between a backdate and a real process start.

- Keep the real-process parts where they prove something only a real process can, and keep them off any timing edge.
