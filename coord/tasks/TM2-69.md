# TM2-69 · bt-service · average holding time metric is wrong
Branch: tm2/TM2-69        Base: main        Owner: TM2

## Finding (real backtest through Claude CLI on the GCP deploy, 2026-11-28)
A 9/21 SMA crossover on BTCUSDT, 1h bars from Binance, 2026-09-29 → 2026-11-28, with 40 round trips, reported an average holding time of **16.2 days**. That's impossible: 40 trades in 60 days, mostly short. The other metrics looked consistent (return −26.75%, win rate 27.5%, max DD −28.3%).

## Files you may touch
- The metrics module that computes holding time (find it: grep for "holding" in `bt-service/vibe_bt`). At most 2 source files.
- `bt-service/tests/test_tm2_69_holding_time.py` (new)

## What to do
- Reproduce it with a deterministic fixture: synthetic 1h bars with known entry and exit times.
- Find the unit or aggregation bug. Likely suspects:
  - bars counted as days on intraday intervals;
  - ns/us confusion after pandas 3;
  - averaging over the wrong denominator;
  - open-to-close spans measured across the whole position history.
- Fix it so the metric is correct for 1m, 1h, 1D and crypto 7-day calendars. Report it in days with fractional precision (or state the unit), consistent with the API docs.

## Acceptance criteria
- Fixtures with known holding periods, e.g. 3h, 5h and 2 days, give exact averages.
- The previously failing case gives a plausible value.
- No other metric changes. Pin the others in the test.
