## Context
- Options symbols such as `NIFTY28NOV2524000CE@NFO` currently route to `india_equity`. That's wrong: it applies no lot size and no expiry.
- Templates (read the class skeletons only):
  - `bt-service/vibe_bt/backtest/engines/options_portfolio.py`
  - `india_fno.py`: lot sizes, F&O cost stack, `fno_root`
- Rates (verified Nov 2026; mark "verify current" in comments):
  - options STT: 0.15% of premium on sell
  - stamp: 0.003% of premium on buy
  - exchange charge on premium

## What to build
- Long and short options positions in lots, using `india_fno`'s lot table, the `lot_sizes` override and its root parsing.
- Premium-based P&L from the option's own price series. No pricing model is needed.
- Expiry:
  - parse it from the symbol, in both the `28NOV25` and `25NOV` forms;
  - on expiry day, settle at intrinsic value against the underlying's close if the config `underlying: {OPTION_ROOT: "<underlying symbol>"}` maps the root and that series is in `data_map`;
  - otherwise settle at the last price;
  - the trade reason is `expiry`.
- Short margin: config `opt_short_margin_pct`, default 0.15 of underlying notional. Cut positions to fit.
- Costs: the rates above plus brokerage and GST as in F&O, all overridable via `in_opt_*`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Routing:
  - CE/PE on NFO/BFO goes to `india_options`;
  - futures stay on `india_fno`;
  - equities are unchanged.
- Long CE P&L = (exit − entry) × lot × lots − costs, hand-checked on one round trip.
- Short PE: margin is enforced and the position is cut to fit.
- At expiry, an ITM call settles at intrinsic value and an OTM call settles at 0.
- An unknown root gets lot 1 plus a warning.
- The full suite stays green.
