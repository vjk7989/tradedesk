# TM2-23 report: optional short-option qty risk cap

## Change
- `config.ts`: `maxShortOptionQtyPerOrder: posInt.optional()` in `riskSchema` (only change).
- `risk/engine.ts`: new additive check in `checkOrder`, placed after the quantity check. Applies only when the key is set, side is SELL, exchange is NFO or BFO and the symbol ends in CE or PE (symbol, exchange and side normalised with trim and upper-case). Reason: `short option qty <q> exceeds maxShortOptionQtyPerOrder <n>`.
- No existing check changed or reordered. Off by default.
- Tests: `trade-mcp/test/tm2_23_short_option_cap.test.ts` (clock pinned via `now`).

## Limitation (conservative close)
The engine has no position knowledge, so a SELL that closes an existing long option position is capped the same as a fresh short. Closing a large long may need splitting into orders at or under the cap (or raising the cap).

## risk.example.json
The key is intentionally NOT added to `deploy/risk.example.json`; it stays off by default. Operators opt in by adding `"maxShortOptionQtyPerOrder": <positive int>`.

## api-docs line (owner adds in O-3)
`maxShortOptionQtyPerOrder` (optional, positive integer, off by default): refuse SELL orders on NFO/BFO option symbols (ending CE/PE) whose quantity exceeds this value. Also applies to SELLs that close longs.
