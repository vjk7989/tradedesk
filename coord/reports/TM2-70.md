## Per-loader rule (naive index = already market-local, as today)
| Loader | Market tz | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `_ffill_pit` (SEC) | America/New_York | EDGAR `filed` is an Eastern calendar date. Tz-aware bars -> ET wall clock. Tz-suffixed `filed` -> `max(stated wall-clock date, ET date of the instant)`: never earlier than either reading, so it can only delay, never advance, visibility. |
| industry | Asia/Shanghai | Tushare `in_date`/`out_date` are CN-local dates; comparing the UTC epoch of an aware index against them shifted every boundary by 8h. |
| rsshub | Asia/Shanghai | `knowable_date` uses the A-share close cutoff (16h) so it is a CN-local date. `_knowable_date` (pubDate) is deliberately unchanged: tests/test_tm11_edge.py pins "wall time is kept" for tz-aware pubDate. `as_of` aware: `min(displayed date, CN date)`, so a boundary east of CN cannot admit tomorrow's items; west-of-CN behaviour unchanged. |

## Guard design
`pit_calendar.assert_clean_datetime_index` is called after every key construction/union: it raises `PitCalendarError` (RuntimeError) if the index is not a `DatetimeIndex` (object dtype), is tz-aware, or (with `monotonic=True`, used on the `_ffill_pit` union before `ffill`) is not monotonic increasing. `_ffill_pit` checks the filed keys and the union; industry and rsshub check their bar keys. tz-aware indexes with repeated wall-clock times (DST fall-back) use unique keys for the union and are re-reindexed onto the caller's exact index; naive path is untouched (still raises on duplicate labels exactly as main did).
