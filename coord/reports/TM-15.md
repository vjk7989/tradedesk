## Cause and fix
On pandas 2.3.3 every `pd.Timedelta("<str>")` / `pd.Timedelta(days=..)` here raised the numpy generic-unit DeprecationWarning (all 12 `_RESAMPLE_RULES` values are str like "3min"/"1h"/"1D" and all warned). `pd.to_timedelta(rule)` also warns; `pd.Timedelta(to_offset(rule))` is equal but has unit "s", not "ns".
- local_loader.py: `target = pd.Timedelta(to_offset(rule).nanos, unit="ns")` (equal value, ns unit, verified for all 9 distinct rules; source/target comparisons unchanged).
- india_options.py:223: `pd.Timedelta(days=1)` -> `pd.Timedelta(1, unit="D")` (identical Timedelta, ns).
The `.diff().dropna().median()` line does not warn.
