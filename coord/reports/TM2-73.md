## Deviation: schedule
The brief asks for "every 30 min". The schema cannot express it: a job has `schedule.days` (list of Mon..Sun) and ONE `time` HH:MM, `tz` is only "Asia/Kolkata" or "UTC" (agent-runner/src/jobs.ts:10-14, :46-52; nextRun picks one time per day, src/scheduler.ts:4-15). Only one job is allowed, so the example runs daily at 00:00 UTC; the README says how to get a 30-min cadence (more jobs, or `--once` from cron). A schema change would need a source edit, out of scope.
